# THREE EXEMPLAR PLAY SESSIONS — HOW THE DESIGN SHOULD FEEL

## Example A — New duo, Cadet, Days 1–3
Two friends spawn in a small orbital departure lounge. A sign reads `BOARD YOUR CREW SHIP`. They invite each other, choose Cadet, ready and launch. They appear near Central Junction; forward window reveals black stars, a ship clock says Day 1. A simple amber warning points to Life Support. Friend A follows the `LIFE SUPPORT` sign to discover a clogged filter; the interface explains a three-source oxygen mixer (Basic). Friend B retrieves one membrane from Cargo and delivers it. Repair completes, oxygen bar visibly stabilizes, ambient hum returns. Day 2 offers easy salvage at a cargo receiver. Day 3 introduces reactor phase tuning; players learn to split tasks. Both receive team XP; no game-over for the first mistake.

## Example B — Four-player Standard, Day 32
Crew arrives in Ion Corridor. Scanner warns of interference, letting players choose between charging shields or risking richer salvage. Two choose shield tuning and power allocation; one crafts coolant cartridge; one launches a drone for debris. A coil fault increases heat, so the technician throttles reactor temporarily, buying time to complete coolant puzzle. The ship enters next day with less power but extra materials; the team discusses choices, not random deaths. No player is forced to idle.

## Example C — Six-player Veteran, Day 100 -> Day 101
At Day 97, players learned that a transmitter component and navigation fragments were needed, and gathered them through sector tasks. At Day 100, a visible three-stage final route mission starts: navigation alignment, coordinated power+cooling stabilization, beacon broadcast. The six crew members split into teams. An unexpected shielding problem is added but does not cancel required tasks or make finale unwinnable. Final signal transmits, the ship's Bridge displays `MISSION COMPLETE`. Each player gets Day-100 achievement. Voting: four players choose Continue and two Extract. Server records completion; run enters `ENDLESS VOYAGE — DAY 101` with capped difficulty, new modifiers and optional extraction after future milestones. No day counter resets.

## Example D — Three players mid-run disconnect
A cargo player disconnects during a required power bus task. The server releases their task reservation and rebalances task capacity for two players. The remaining players can complete the repair in sequence, not lose instantly. The returner can rejoin if party/run allows and capacity permits, without restoring previously spent parts or duplicating XP. An interrupted teleport does not strand them at a black screen indefinitely.

## What an unacceptable session looks like
Giant flat lobby with random neon platforms, a ship exterior under blue daylight, a maze with no room labels, life support bar decreasing with no repair option, twelve copied single-click minigames, group forced to watch a four-hour timer without gameplay, six-person-only switches in a two-player run, Day 100 showing only `You win` then stopping, or a broken pause/resume claim. All violate the specification.
